Innovation and Trial and Error

Our system turns an ordinary laptop webcam into a CPR evaluator. MediaPipe pose and hand tracking, running right in the web browser, checks locked elbows and stacked hands and measures the compression rate against the recommended 100 to 120 per minute, with live coaching like "Push faster" or "Lock your elbows." It also guides trainees step by step through the 2025 American Heart Association sequence: checking the scene, calling 911, giving 2 breaths after every 30 compressions, and using an AED. Everything runs offline on one laptop, with a low-cost DIY chest prop and a local dashboard that tracks progress. No video ever leaves the computer. Through repeated testing, we calibrated thresholds and adjusted for lighting and camera position.

 Screen recording of the Live Session: green locked-arm overlay, gold finger tracking, the coaching cue (“Push faster”), the step-by-step CPR panel showing “Give 2 breaths” and the AED step; then the Dashboard and a trainee’s My Progress page; the DIY prop; a quick shot of calibration in Settings.
