A. Functional suitability54321
FS1The system correctly shows whether my elbows are locked (arms straight) during compressions.
FS2The system correctly shows my compression rate and tells me when to push faster or slow down.
FS3The step-by-step guide follows the correct adult CPR sequence (scene, response, breathing, call 911, compressions, breaths, AED).
FS4The session results, reports and certificate contain the information I need.
B. Performance efficiency54321
PE1The feedback on the screen appears without noticeable delay.
PE2The camera view and body tracking run smoothly on the computer used.
PE3The system starts and is ready to use within an acceptable time.
PE4Saving a session and opening results and reports is fast.
C. Usability54321
US1The system is easy to learn to use.
US2The on-screen messages and colors are easy to understand while doing compressions.
US3The buttons, menus and pages are clearly labeled and easy to find.
US4The feedback helped me improve my CPR technique.
D. Reliability54321
RE1The system worked without crashing or freezing during my sessions.
RE2My session results were saved correctly.
RE3The system kept working when the trainee briefly moved out of the camera view.
RE4The system gives consistent results when the same technique is repeated.
E. Security54321
SE1Only people with an account can sign in to the system.
SE2Trainees can see only their own results.
SE3I am confident that the camera video is not recorded or stored.
SE4Trainee information is kept private and protected by passwords.
F. Portability54321
PO1The system can be set up easily on another computer.
PO2The system works in common web browsers (Chrome or Edge).
PO3The system works without an internet connection once installed on the laptop.
PO4The system works with an ordinary built-in or USB webcam.
